# Webtoon authoring guidance

Plan the artwork, dialogue and reading sequence together. These are authoring
considerations, not measured genre rules or a guarantee of image quality.

## Composition

Choose each cut's narrative purpose, framing and visible subjects. Reserve room
for likely lettering while keeping faces, gestures and important objects clear.
A close-up, establishing view and action shot need different compositions.
Do not resize every cut to the same tall shape to solve a layout problem.

## Lettering

Make speech, narration, thought and sound effects distinguishable. Keep speech
attribution and reading order clear. Judge legibility at the intended reading
width. If text cannot fit at a readable size, revise the words or the available
space. Do not hide an overflow problem by shrinking indefinitely.

## Sequence

Use changes in framing, scale and spacing to support the story. A transition
should have a deliberate narrative purpose. Passing a lint or measurement check
is not a substitute for reading the result.
