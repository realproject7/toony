# Transitions in an episode

Toony stores transitions as entries in the episode reading sequence. They are
rendered in previews and exports. Current kinds and fields are documented in
[PROJECT_FORMAT.md](./PROJECT_FORMAT.md).

Choose spacing, color, text and artwork according to the scene's reading intent.
A blank gap, a text card and an illustrated transition serve different purposes;
adding variety alone does not make a coherent sequence. Review the full scroll
at the target width, including the join between a cut and its transition.

Keep narration and spoken dialogue distinguishable, and keep important artwork
visible. A measurement describes the rendered result; it does not determine
whether a transition reads naturally.

Detailed craft research, proposed composition features and experiment records
are maintained locally. This document does not imply that shared-source image
cropping or continuous-scene generation is already implemented.
