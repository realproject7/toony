# Commercial licensing

Toony is source-available under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](./LICENSE).
Noncommercial use is granted by that license, free of charge. Commercial use needs
a separate paid license from the copyright holder.

This page explains that line and makes the licensor's offer. Where it describes
the noncommercial license it is a summary, not a grant: if anything here and the
[LICENSE](./LICENSE) text disagree, the LICENSE text wins. The one place this page
grants something on its own is the evaluation permission below, which says so
explicitly.

## Already free under the noncommercial license

You do not need to ask, pay, or register for any of this. The license covers it.

- Drawing and publishing webtoons you do not monetize, including on free
  platforms and personal sites.
- Personal research, experiment, and testing for the benefit of public knowledge,
  where no commercial application is anticipated.
- Personal study, private entertainment, hobby projects, amateur pursuits, and
  religious observance.
- Teaching and learning.
- Reading, modifying, and sharing the source under the license terms.
- Use inside a charitable organization, educational institution, public research
  organization, public safety or health organization, environmental protection
  organization, or government institution. That safe harbour is about **what the
  organization is**, not what the particular project earns, and the license says
  it applies regardless of the source of funding.

This list summarises the license's Noncommercial Purposes, Personal Uses, and
Noncommercial Organizations sections. Read those sections for the operative
wording.

## Evaluation: an additional grant from us

Deciding whether you need a commercial license is, by its nature, a use with an
anticipated commercial application, so the noncommercial license does **not**
cover it. You should still be able to try Toony before paying for it.

So, separately from the PolyForm license, the copyright holder grants you
permission to use Toony for up to 32 consecutive days to evaluate whether it
suits your commercial application, on behalf of you or your company. Anything you
publish or ship commercially still needs a commercial license.

## Needs a commercial license

Buy a commercial license if Toony is part of making money. For example:

- publishing episodes that earn revenue, through paid platforms, ad revenue,
  reader payments, subscriptions, or sponsorship
- commissioned or client work, including freelance webtoon production
- studio, agency, or in-house production pipelines
- bundling Toony, or work derived from it, into a product or service you sell
- any other use with an anticipated commercial application, beyond the evaluation
  window above

Scale is not the test, and neither is how the work looks. One person publishing a
monetized series needs a license. A nonprofit named in the safe harbour above does
not, even if the project is funded.

## Not sure

Ask before you ship. Most cases are obvious, and the ones that are not are worth
a short conversation rather than a guess.

## How to ask

Open an issue titled `Commercial license inquiry` on the
[Toony repository](https://github.com/realproject7/toony/issues/new) with a short
description of the work and its scale. The issue tracker is public, so do not put
contracts, budgets, or anything confidential in it; say what you need and we will
take the details off the public thread.

## What a commercial license covers

Terms are set per engagement, based on the scale and shape of the work. A
commercial license grants the commercial rights that the noncommercial license
withholds **in Toony itself**. It does not relicense third-party material that
ships alongside it: the bundled fonts stay under their own SIL Open Font License
terms, documented in
[`packages/fonts/assets/README.md`](./packages/fonts/assets/README.md). The OFL
permits commercial use, so this is a matter of whose license governs, not a gap
in your rights.

A commercial license does not change the warranty position. Toony ships as is,
with no warranty, exactly as the LICENSE states.
