# Commercial licensing

Toony is source-available under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](./LICENSE).
Every noncommercial use is already granted by that license, free of charge.
Commercial use needs a separate paid license from the copyright holder.

This page is a plain-language guide to that line. It explains the license, it does
not change it. If anything here and the [LICENSE](./LICENSE) text disagree, the
LICENSE text wins.

## Free under the noncommercial license

You do not need to ask, pay, or register to:

- draw and publish webtoons you do not monetize, including on free platforms and
  personal sites
- learn, study, or teach with Toony, including in schools and courses
- evaluate Toony before deciding whether you need a commercial license
- run it for hobby, fan, amateur, or personal creative work
- research, experiment, and test with it
- read, modify, and share the source under the license terms
- use it inside a charity, school, public research body, or government
  institution, whatever their funding

## Needs a commercial license

Buy a commercial license if Toony is part of making money, for example:

- publishing episodes that earn revenue, through paid platforms, ad revenue,
  reader payments, subscriptions, or sponsorship
- commissioned or client work, including freelance webtoon production
- studio, agency, or in-house production pipelines
- bundling Toony, or work derived from it, into a product or service you sell
- any other use with an anticipated commercial application

The test is what the work is for, not how big you are. One person publishing a
monetized series needs a license. A large charity running an unpaid community
project does not.

## Not sure

Ask before you ship. Most cases are obvious, and the ones that are not are worth
a short conversation rather than a guess.

## How to ask

Open an issue titled `Commercial license inquiry` on the
[Toony repository](https://github.com/realproject7/toony/issues/new) with a short
description of the work and the scale. Do not include contracts, budgets, or
anything confidential in a public issue; say what you need and we will take the
details off the public thread.

## What a commercial license covers

Terms are set per engagement, based on the scale and shape of the work. A
commercial license grants the commercial rights the noncommercial license
withholds. It does not change the warranty position: Toony ships as is, with no
warranty, exactly as the LICENSE states.
